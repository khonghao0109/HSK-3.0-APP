# Content Moderation & Trust & Safety

1. Viết policy theo loại harm, jurisdiction/age, enforcement level và transparent examples; legal review khi cần.
2. Content ingest có provenance/license, automated scan/classification + human review theo risk; không auto-publish AI critical content.
3. Workflow pending/approved/rejected/quarantined/appealed; separation of duties, immutable review/audit và reason code safe.
4. Report/appeal accessible, chống spam/retaliation; priority cho child safety, threats, PII và malware.
5. Moderator least privilege, training/wellbeing, queue SLA/quality sampling và sensitive-data controls.
6. Abuse prevention rate/velocity/device/account signal nhưng có false-positive/fairness review.
7. Monitor prevalence, response time, overturn rate và drift; incident/law-enforcement path được phê duyệt.

**Gate:** no unowned high-severity queue, user notification/appeal rõ, audit/retention/privacy và kill switch.
